01 / SERVICES

What we build

Five kinds of systems, each scoped and built around how your business actually operates.

01

Custom business platforms

Purpose-built web apps for how you actually work — order management, staff tools, internal dashboards — instead of forcing your process into a generic template.

02

E-commerce & marketplaces

Storefronts with real checkout logic, WhatsApp or card payments, inventory sync, and an admin panel you can actually use — not a page of products with no back end.

03

Booking & scheduling systems

Appointment and reservation flows with availability logic, reminders and staff calendars built around your actual business hours and services.

04

Dashboards & admin panels

A single place to see and manage what's happening in the business — orders, customers, staff, reports — instead of five spreadsheets and a notebook.

05

Ongoing support & maintenance

Hosting, updates, fixes and small improvements after launch, so the system keeps working as your business grows and changes.
